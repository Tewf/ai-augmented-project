# 0002: Keep the name, and take the maintainer's practice as baseline

**Decision:** The repository keeps its name after the frame step, and the baseline of its first review is the maintainer's own practice, written down as B1.

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-02. **Shape:** [decision record](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/65e42472e2d4cbfd994c2a86e95a47e5dc7301ca/shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- The frame step found the field's words: human-AI teaming, hybrid intelligence, agentic coding, agent harness ([frame](../evidence/human-ai-teaming/frame.md)).
- "AI-augmented" echoes augmentation, the field's word for a team that beats the person alone.
- The review found no published setup to compare against: studies measure single parts (context files, speed), not a whole setup.
- The review is finished only when it names a baseline.

## Decision

- Keep the name. The mismatch is partial, and a rename moves the address for no gain.
- Name the baseline B1: five practices of the maintainer, described without tools, paths or private repositories.
- Name the evidence topic human-ai-teaming, the field's word.

## Consequences

- A reader can compare any setup with B1 and with the studies that bear on each of its parts.
- **Bad:** B1 is one person's practice, with no control and no measure; it is a baseline to be measured, not a proven one.
- The design document must supply the measure ([position](../evidence/human-ai-teaming/position.md)).
