# The model decides nothing unchecked

**Claim:** a program decides what it can check, the person decides the rest, and the model drafts and proposes without deciding.

- **Grade:** measured for tasks that decide, where a person with a model scored below the better of the two (Vaccaro 2024). The split of owners among the three parties is a convention.
- **Evidence:** [review](../../evidence/human-ai-teaming/review.md), strand 1 (Vaccaro 2024; Bansal 2021); [state of the art](../../evidence/human-ai-teaming/state-of-the-art.md), question 1; [design](../../design/one-project.md), decision 1; [record 0003](../../decisions/0003-the-model-decides-nothing-unchecked.md).
- **Check:** `brief-checks` decides the half a program can: a task with no check to judge it is refused. Who decided each choice is none yet: judgment; a program could count the answers in a decision log, which is not built.
- **Reader styles:** none.

## What it asks

- The person decides what is built and which checks count. The machine decides pass or fail. The table of who decides what is in [the design](../../design/one-project.md).
- A draft reaches the project only after a check passes or the person reads it.
- The model repairs after a failed check, and asks when no check covers the action.

## Why

- Across 106 experiments, a person with a model scored below the better of the two alone, Hedges g = -0.23. Tasks that decide lost; tasks that create gained (Vaccaro 2024).
- Explanations raised the acceptance of wrong answers (Bansal 2021).
- The rule is the one the [blueprint](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-blueprint/blob/f9b2408e7f1ddf4a82d2d480c62d6b5855e14e0b/README.md) holds for reasoning, carried to a whole project.

## Limits

- The meta-analysis averages over many tasks and models. It does not say that this person's tasks lose.
- Whether a choice is one that decides is itself a judgment. The rule offers no program for it.
- The sources are studies of people and models in general, not of the maintainer's work.
